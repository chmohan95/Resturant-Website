About Us

Founded with a passion for India's rich culinary heritage, The Great Indian Kitchen brings together time-honored family recipes from across the subcontinent. Every morning, our chefs hand-roast and grind whole spices in-house, slow-simmer our gravies from scratch, and bake fresh breads in our traditional clay tandoor to serve you an authentic, unforgettable dining experience.
